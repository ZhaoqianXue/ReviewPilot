# Evidence Screening

## Screening procedure
1. Identify what the study itself did: its design, what or whom it studied, and the kind of data or results it reports.
2. Compare that with the review focus and the eligibility criteria given in the prompt. The review focus explains what the criteria are for: judge whether the record's own work could supply the information the review collects.
3. Exclude when the record's own work clearly falls in an excluded category or could not supply that information. A topic mentioned only as background, motivation or an example does not count.
4. Rate how likely the record is to be eligible. Use "uncertain" when the title and abstract leave the decision open; use "probably exclude" or "definitely exclude" only when the record's own work points outside the review focus or criteria.
5. Give a one-sentence reason that names the deciding rule.
