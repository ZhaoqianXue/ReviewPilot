## Scope and retained limitations

Removal reasons absent from historical screening runs cannot be reconstructed reliably. Older projects must be screened again to produce a complete per-record ledger. PDFs without historical download provenance explicitly show unknown.

Date coverage still depends on each source's date index and the selected result limit. arXiv uses first-version submission dates. Screening checks completeness only for returned records; it cannot guarantee retrieval of unknown-date records omitted by a source index. Parameter contracts follow [NCBI ESearch dates](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK25499/), [arXiv date queries](https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/user-manual.html#query_details) and [OpenAlex date filtering](https://help.openalex.org/api/filtering/). OpenAlex grouped queries follow its [native Boolean search documentation](https://help.openalex.org/api/searching/).

Full Results currently supplies all records at once, suitable for the 70-paper scale tested here. Browser performance for tens of thousands of records remains unverified. P2 issues G11–G13 were outside this iteration; G10's explicit search-save entry was added with G01.
